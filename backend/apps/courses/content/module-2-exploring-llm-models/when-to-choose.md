# When to Choose Each Model

Choosing a model is usually about matching its strengths to your goals.

## Choose Claude when

- You want polished, natural writing help.
- You are working on summarization, editing, or brainstorming tasks.
- You value a model that often feels conversational and less boilerplate-heavy.

## Choose Gemini when

- Your work involves images, video, or other multimodal inputs.
- You want strong integration with Google tools and ecosystems.
- You need a model that was designed early around multimodal understanding.

## Choose OpenAI models when

- You need a strong general-purpose assistant for chat, coding, and tool use.
- You want a reliable default choice for many everyday AI tasks.
- You are building applications that benefit from broad ecosystem support.

## Choose open-source models when

- You want to run models locally or privately.
- You need more control over cost, deployment, or customization.
- You are experimenting with research, fine-tuning, or self-hosted systems.

## A simple decision checklist

Ask yourself:

1. Do I need strong writing quality?
2. Do I need image or video understanding?
3. Do I need coding support or agent-style tool use?
4. Do I need local control or privacy?

The answers to those questions usually point to the right model family.
