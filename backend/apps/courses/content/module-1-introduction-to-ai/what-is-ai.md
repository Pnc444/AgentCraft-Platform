# What is AI?

A working definition of artificial intelligence — and why "narrow" is the word doing the heavy lifting.

**Watch for three things:**

- The high-level definition: computers doing things that normally require human intelligence
- Why large datasets matter — spotting patterns, making predictions, recommending actions
- The AlphaGo example, and the limitation raised immediately after it

That last point is the one most people skip past. AlphaGo beat a legendary Go professional, and the same system still could not drive a car, walk, or play Monopoly. Superhuman at one task is not general intelligence.
