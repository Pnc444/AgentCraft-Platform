# Brief History

Seventy years of AI: two booms, two winters, and one release that changed the conversation.

**Watch for the timeline:**

- Turing's 1950 question — "Can machines think?"
- 1956 at Dartmouth, where the term *artificial intelligence* was coined
- Why the first AI winter hit: big promises, critical reports, funding gone
- What changed in the late 1990s — far stronger computers and far more digital data

Notice the pattern. Progress stalls when it runs on promises and restarts when the hardware and data catch up. That cycle is worth remembering when you hear claims about what agents will do next year.
